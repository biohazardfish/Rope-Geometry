Maximum discrepancy between kernels on these fixtures was 1.12e-16 m.
This validates agreement on those inputs, not a mathematical theorem about
arbitrary floating-point inputs, a smooth tube, or knot identity.

The historical distance kernel drops the interior candidate for sufficiently
parallel segments: `[0,0,0]→[1,0,0]` and
`[0,-1e-8,0]→[1,1e-8,0]` intersect, but it reports about 1e-8 m.
`robust-distance.js` uses cross products instead of subtracting nearly equal
squared dot products. Regression tests cover this intersection at scales
1e-6, 1 and 1e6, as well as parallel, degenerate, endpoint and skew cases.
It remains floating-point numerical computation, not certified interval
arithmetic. The frozen historical engine and baseline hashes are preserved.

## Evidence boundary

The final reference photo was inspected again: it does not expose the rear
cuff connections or all individual loop-root connections. No complete source
traversal was inferred from it. Missing evidence remains a labelled E0/E1
trace, rear/underside limb pairing, both sides of the loosened non-slipped
locking core, and all FINAL crossings in one specified projection. A Gauss
word alone would also not establish equivalence relative to the column.

The geometric object is a polygonal centerline with capsule primitives, not
a proven embedded smooth material tube. Immediate segment adjacency is the
only historical exclusion. Subdivision changes that exclusion set; overlap
counts therefore need not remain constant even along a straight rope.
Neither a successful build nor passing expected-failure tests establishes a
completed Somerville Bowline.
