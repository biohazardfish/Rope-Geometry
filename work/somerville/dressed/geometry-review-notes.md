# Codex independent geometry review notes

- A 6 mm paired passage needs at least 6.2 mm centre separation. If first and second passage centres differ along aperture U, both bundles must not also be separated along U with overlapping interior strands. A starting packing is passage centres ±3.8 mm along U, paired limbs ±3.1 mm along V. This is a metric initialization, not source strand-order evidence.
- For four passage strands arranged in a 6.2 mm square, centre envelope radius is about 4.38 mm. Adding their 3 mm radius, 0.2 mm clearance, and a 3 mm lock-rim radius gives a minimal circular rim centre radius around 10.6 mm. Thus 11 mm is close to a geometric lower bound, not a comfortably loose initial loop.
- A doubled lock rim separated along its normal reduces the in-plane packing conflict but requires checking frame transport into/out of the cuff and actual surface gaps. No world-left permutation should be inferred from this geometric choice.
- With front direction −Y, aperture radial normal n=[0,−cos(theta),−sin(theta)] and U=[1,0,0], one consistent V=[0,−sin(theta),cos(theta)] gives U cross V=n. Core location and ring-plane tilt may be independent, but tests must use the actual exported frame.
